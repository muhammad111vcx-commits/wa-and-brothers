How to wear attar

Attar is an oil, so a little goes a long way.

  1. Roll it on your wrists

    Roll gently on the inner wrists. Warm skin carries the scent.

  2. Then on your shirt

    A touch on the collar or cuff keeps the fragrance with you through the day.

  3. Close the cap firmly

    Twist until it stops, and keep the bottle upright and out of direct sun.
